A solo-developed mobile game for Android and iOS that took about 6 months part time in my spare time.

Partly created for the challenge and experience of publishing a game, partly because I wanted a WipEout 'Zone' style game to play on the bus...

> Features:
> - Music-reative environment and colours
> - All diegetic race UI
> - 5 Unique environments and 10 unlockable tracks
> - Achievements and Leaderboards using Google Play and GameCenter
> - Low quality settings for older devices to maximise audience
> - Simple 2 finger controls

"Neon takes you on a high speed survival challenge. Inspired by favourite anti-gravity racers of the past, now you can get that solo experience on your mobile!"

(App removed from stores in 2023 due to depricated APIs)
